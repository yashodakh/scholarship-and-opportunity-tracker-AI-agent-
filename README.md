# Scholarship and Opportunity Tracker

Scholarship and Opportunity Tracker is an AI-powered platform that helps students discover, track, and manage scholarships, internships, fellowships, grants, competitions, and career opportunities. The system analyzes user profiles, academic backgrounds, skills, and interests to provide personalized recommendations and guidance.

The platform simplifies the opportunity search process by matching students with relevant programs based on eligibility criteria, field of study, and career goals. It also helps users stay informed through deadline tracking, application reminders, and AI-powered assistance.

With an integrated chatbot, students can explore opportunities, understand application requirements, receive personalized suggestions, and get answers to their queries in real time. The platform aims to improve access to educational and professional opportunities while reducing the time and effort required for manual searches.
